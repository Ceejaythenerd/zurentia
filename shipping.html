SHIPPING & DELIVERY

Local Delivery

We deliver nationwide across South Africa. Standard delivery takes approximately 3-5 working days from the date your order is dispatched. We partner with reliable couriers to ensure your botanical goods arrive safely.

Delivery Costs

  • Standard Delivery: R100 nationwide.
  • Free Delivery: Orders over R1000 automatically qualify for free standard shipping.

Order Processing

Because our products are handcrafted in small batches to ensure freshness and potency, please allow 1-2 business days for your order to be prepared before it is shipped.

Collection

If you are based locally, you may opt to collect your order directly from us to save on shipping fees. Simply select "Collection" at checkout and we will send you a WhatsApp message to coordinate a collection time.

International Shipping

At this time, we only ship within South Africa. We hope to bring our healing rituals to the rest of the world very soon. Join our Ritual Circle newsletter to be notified of any updates.
