FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are your products completely natural?

Yes. We pride ourselves on using 100% natural, sustainably sourced botanical ingredients. We never use synthetic fragrances, parabens, or artificial colors in our formulations.

How long do your products last?

Because we don't use chemical preservatives, our products have a natural shelf life of approximately 6 to 12 months. We recommend keeping them out of direct sunlight and in a cool, dry place to maintain their potency.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

Our ingredients are chosen for their gentle, healing properties, making them generally safe for all skin types. However, because botanicals are potent, we recommend doing a small patch test before full use if you have highly sensitive skin or known plant allergies.

How often should I use the botanical body oil?

For best results, we recommend applying our body oils daily, ideally right after a warm bath or shower when your skin is slightly damp, allowing it to deeply absorb the nourishing oils.
